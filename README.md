
# Supply Chain Spend & Risk Optimization Dashboard

## Project Overview
This project focuses on identifying procurement risks and optimizing operational spend using simulated supply chain transactional data. By combining data manipulation techniques with automated calculations, the dashboard isolates variations in supplier delivery timelines and high-risk financial exposure.

## Key Excel Skills Demonstrated
* **Advanced Lookups:** Utilized `XLOOKUP` to dynamically bridge siloed transactional logs with supplier master databases.
* **Data Engineering & Analysis:** Configured statistical formulas to measure On-Time Delivery (OTD) rates and isolate supply chain lead-time variances.
* **Dynamic Reporting:** Structured relational Pivot Tables and cross-connected interactive Slicers to build a fully automated, scannable operational dashboard.

## Strategic Business Insights Revealed
1. Identified that high-risk international freight routes averaged a **3.5-day delivery variance** compared to low-risk regional packaging suppliers.
2. isolated peak category expenditures, allowing procurement to target the top 20% of suppliers driving 80% of logistical budget allocation.
